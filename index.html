How KIN earns trust

Four pillars, named on every listing:

Recipient choice

A listing is published only with the recipient's consent.

KIN decision

KIN decides eligibility, verifies the need, and approves each listing before it appears.

Partner choice

Partners choose how they take part; services stay independent.

Supporter choice

Supporters choose which verified need to meet. That choice is theirs.
