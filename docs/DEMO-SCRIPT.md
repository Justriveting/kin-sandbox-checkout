# Demo video script (target 2:30, hard cap under 3:00)
Shoot by Tue Nov 10, 2026. Screen recording, Tayo narrating or captions.

0:00 Problem (20s). Single mothers often have to tell their hardest story in public
to get help with ordinary costs. KIN removes that step.
0:20 The page (30s). Show the honesty banner first: fictional examples, no real
donations, PayPal sandbox. Then the three anonymized listings: "Confidential
household," metro only, no names, no faces.
0:50 Trust model (30s). Name the four pillars on screen: recipient choice, KIN
decision, partner choice, supporter choice.
1:20 AI explainer (30s). Open listing kin-demo-001, trigger the explainer live, read
its first line aloud: what the payment covers, what KIN verified, what KIN does not decide.
1:50 Sandbox checkout (40s). Support the listing, complete PayPal sandbox checkout,
land on the confirmation with the sandbox transaction ID. Say the line: "Sandbox
mode. No real money moved, and that is the point of this prototype."
2:30 Close (20s). Tagline on screen: "A little help can change everything." One line
on what a real pilot adds next: verified partner fulfillment, still no public appeals.
